Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 May 2026

Let's keep this short. I'm one person who built a free tool. I have zero interest in your personal data.

Who I am

Carl Manson, based in Australia. If you need to get in touch: carl.manson@westnet.com.au

What data I collect

Almost nothing. The full list:

  • Google Analytics — If you consent, I use Google Analytics (GA4, measurement ID G-4D64BX6X5R) to see how many people use the tool and which features they use. This sets cookies. I don't use it for advertising, remarketing, or anything creepy. If you decline, GA never loads — no tracking at all.
  • localStorage — The app saves your settings and uploaded image in your browser's localStorage so it persists between sessions. This data never leaves your device. It's not sent anywhere.

That's it. No accounts, no email collection, no contact forms, no server-side logging, no third-party trackers, no advertising pixels.

Your images

Any image you upload is processed entirely in your browser using a hidden HTML canvas. It is never uploaded to a server. A copy may be stored locally in localStorage so it persists on refresh.

Cookies

The only cookies on this site come from Google Analytics, and only if you click "Fine by me" on the consent banner. If you decline, no cookies are set. Your consent preference is stored in localStorage (not a cookie) under the key cp_cookie_consent.

Google Analytics details

When active, GA collects:

  • Pages visited
  • Approximate location (country/city level, from IP — I don't see your IP)
  • Device type, browser, and screen size

Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics (GA4) — consent required, as described above
  • Bootstrap & Bootstrap Icons — loaded from jsDelivr CDN. jsDelivr privacy policy

No other third-party services. No ads, no social media widgets, no embedded content.

Your rights (GDPR & Australian Privacy Act)

  • Refuse cookies — click "No thanks" on the banner. GA won't load.
  • Withdraw consent — use the button below to reset your preference.
  • Delete your data — clear your browser's localStorage for this site. For GA data, contact me and I'll delete it.
  • Access your data — contact me. Spoiler: it's basically nothing.
